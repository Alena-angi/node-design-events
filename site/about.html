[ 01 ] · About NØDE

Ideas needother minds.

We started NØDE because the best part of a design event is rarely the slides. It is the people in the room — and what happens when they meet.

Scroll ↓

[ 02 ]

What is NØDE?What is NØDE?

NØDE is a home for design events — workshops, talks, meetups and reviews for UI/UX, product and digital designers. We put the event and the people around it on the same page, so going to a talk can also mean meeting the person you will build something with next.

[ 03 ]

Why we existWhy we exist

  1. 01Discover

    Find the right room

    Curated events from NØDE and partners, online and in person, with clear dates, times and prices — so you spend less time searching and more time learning.

  2. 02Connect

    Meet people, not profiles

    Events are where real conversations happen. NØDE is built around showing up, asking questions and staying in touch with the people you meet there.

  3. 03Grow

    Get better, together

    Critique, reviews and hands-on sessions help you sharpen your craft — and see how other designers think, work and solve problems.
