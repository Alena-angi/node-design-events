[ 03 ] · FAQ

Got questions?We’ve gotanswers.

What kinds of events are on NØDE?

Workshops, talks, meetups, clinics and portfolio reviews for UI/UX, product and digital designers — online, in person and hybrid.

What is the difference between NØDE Original and Partner events?

NØDE Originals are run by the NØDE team: you register right here. Partner events are run by other organisers: we link you to their site to register or buy tickets.

Are the events free?

Many are. Every card shows the price up front — use the Price filter to see only free events.

Which time zone are the times in?

Online events show your local time. In-person and hybrid events show the venue’s local time, with the zone next to it.

How do I register?

Open an event and press Register (NØDE Originals) or Get tickets (Partner events). Your NØDE registrations appear in My Pass.

Can I list my own event?

Yes — use Submit an Event. Every submission is reviewed by hand before it appears in the catalog.
