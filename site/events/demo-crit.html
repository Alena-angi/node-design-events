About the event

A live critique of interaction design work: flows, states, edge cases and the details in between.

Who it’s for

  • Interaction and UX designers
  • Anyone with a flow they want feedback on

What you’ll get

  • Direct feedback on a flow you bring
  • A shared vocabulary for critique
  • Patterns for empty, error and loading states
